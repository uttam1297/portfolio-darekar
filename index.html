Optimizing Net Working Capital: Strategic Financial Analysis (Pharma)

Hochschule für Technik und Wirtschaft Berlin

Problem

Global pharmaceutical companies face significant capital lock-up due to inefficient supply chains and extended payment terms.

Approach

Conducted a financial deep-dive across 10 global industry leaders, utilizing DSO, inventory turns, AR turns, and cash return ratios to establish a quantitative baseline.

Analysis

Built a weighted scoring model to rank performance, identifying clear discrepancies between top performers and lower-quartile organizations facing liquidity traps.

Recommendations

Formulated robust strategies aimed at freeing cash flow, streamlining collections, mitigating capital costs, and expanding operational liquidity.

Key Learnings

Data-driven benchmarking is essential for aligning working capital efficiency with broader corporate strategy, directly impacting growth capacity without external funding.
