Dec 2024

Optimizing Net Working Capital: Strategic Financial Analysis (Pharma)

Hochschule für Technik und Wirtschaft Berlin

Problem

Global pharmaceutical companies face significant capital lock-up due to inefficient supply chains and extended payment terms.

Approach

Conducted a financial deep-dive across 10 global industry leaders, utilizing DSO, inventory turns, AR turns, and cash return ratios to establish a quantitative baseline.

Analysis

Built a weighted scoring model to rank performance, identifying clear discrepancies between top performers and lower-quartile organizations facing liquidity traps.

Recommendations

Formulated robust strategies aimed at freeing cash flow, streamlining collections, mitigating capital costs, and expanding operational liquidity.

Key Learnings

Data-driven benchmarking is essential for aligning working capital efficiency with broader corporate strategy, directly impacting growth capacity without external funding.

Financial analysis Benchmarking Scoring models Cash-flow optimization Corporate finance

Bombardier C-Series (A220): Project Turnaround & Delivery Insights

Hochschule für Technik und Wirtschaft Berlin

Context

The Bombardier C-Series faced massive delays, cost overruns, and severe lifecycle risks that threatened the entire company despite engineering brilliance.

Challenges

Analyzed interconnected risks across severe financial pressures, high technical complexity, brittle supply chains, and demanding stakeholder expectations.

What changed after Airbus

Assessed the vital intervention and turnaround strategy: ruthless cost optimization, targeted rebranding (to A220), and significant execution improvements driven by scale.

Lessons for Product/Ops Leaders

Technical superiority cannot compensate for operational and financial fragility. Managing the full product lifecycle requires integrated planning beyond simple engineering deliverables.

Key Learnings

Produced risk mitigation frameworks and strategy insights using benchmarking and structured project planning techniques.
